Before hitting the complete button
1. Stretching: After a run has ended and after the celebration animations have already played
2. Celebration 1: When you beat your personal best and your run has ended
3. Celebration 2: If you haven't beat your personal best and your run has ended
4. Celebration 3: When your run has ended and you've tied your personal best.
5. Running clean: When you have a streak going and you haven't missed a day.
6. Fall and get up: When you've missed a day, but your streak is still viable.
7. Both running clean: when you're running clean and your past self was running clean.
8. Seeing past mistakes: When you're running clean and your past self stumbled
9. Double mistakes: When you've missed a day and your past run also slipped up.
10. Your mistake: when you've missed a day, but your past run was clean.
11. Reading a book: for rest days and paused days.

After hitting the complete button
1. Running clean: A single figure running clean: after 5, 6, and sometimes 1 if there is no past run
2. Both running clean: After 2, 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 1 if there is a past run to compare against.
3. Show the passing animation with the high five if the completion beats your personal best.
