OELA — third-party notices
==========================

OELA itself is one self-contained HTML file with no third-party JavaScript in
it. This file exists for the two things that ARE bundled and whose licences
require their notice to travel with each copy.

Where each notice lives, and why:

  * Fonts (Inter, Space Grotesk, IBM Plex Mono) are base64-embedded directly
    inside index.html, so their SIL Open Font License 1.1 notice is embedded
    there too, in full, at the top of the first <style> block. That is the copy
    the licence attaches to.

  * Capacitor is compiled into the Android build only. Its MIT notice is below.
    The plain HTML file and the Windows build contain no Capacitor code.

  * The two photographic desk surfaces (Dark wood, Rosewood) come from Poly
    Haven (https://polyhaven.com) and are CC0 — public domain, no attribution
    required. They are credited anyway, in the source, because the work
    deserves naming: Dark Wood by Dimitrios Savva (photography) and Rico
    Cilliers (tiling); Rosewood Veneer1 by Jenelle van Heerden. The other desk
    surfaces are drawn by the app and are original to it.

  * The Windows build bundles Electron and Chromium, which ship their own
    LICENSE and LICENSES.chromium.html files alongside the executable.
