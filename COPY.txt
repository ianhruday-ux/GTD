HOW TO USE THIS FILE
  Read down it. When you want something changed, write the new wording on the
  line underneath it, after the arrow. Leave the arrow line blank (or delete it)
  for anything you are happy with. Don't worry about matching the exact spelling
  of the old text — I find these by their tag, not by their content.

  Example:
      [A12] placeholder   Add to list…
         → What needs doing?

  Each entry's tag (like [A12]) is how I locate it in the code, so please leave
  those alone. The (file:line) at the end is for me.

WHAT THE KINDS MEAN
  button/label   text on a button, a tab, or a heading — you see it as-is
  placeholder    the grey hint inside an empty box, gone as soon as you type
  tooltip        the label on hover, or long-press on a phone
  text           full sentences: info panels, empty states, confirm dialogs
  screen-reader  never shown; only read aloud by a screen reader

  The habit thought-bubbles are under HABITS AND THE RUNNER. Those are the
  ones that will need Chinese versions, so they are worth extra attention.

  NOT IN THIS FILE, on purpose: the QA checklist, the chunk map, the dev
  buttons, and the sample data. Those are scaffolding for building the app —
  they get regenerated every round or stripped from the real build, so editing
  them here would be wasted work.

  A few strings may be filed under the wrong heading — the grouping is worked
  out from the code's own structure, which does not always match how the app
  looks. If something is in an odd place, it is still the right string.
